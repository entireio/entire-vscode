## 4. `exec.ts` and `errors.ts`

**`exec`** (`src/core/exec.ts:50`):
- Signature: `Exec = (file, args, {cwd, timeoutMs, env?, signal?, maxBytes?, stdoutFile?}) =&gt; Promise&lt;ExecResult&gt;`.
- Uses `spawn` with `shell:false`, stdin set to `ignore`, and `detached` on POSIX.
- Timeout or abort sends SIGTERM to the process group, then SIGKILL after 2s.
- Defaults: `maxBytes` 16MB, stderr capped at 64KB.
- The result carries `code`, `timedOut`, `aborted`, `truncated` and `spawnError`.
- Because stdin is ignored, interactive commands like `entire login` can't be run through it.

`EntireCli.run` (entireCli.ts:118-139) adds `NO_COLOR=1` and `GIT_TERMINAL_PROMPT=0` to the environment. It also runs calls through a concurrency lane of 4 with optional dedupe, and logs each call. `version` has a 5s timeout and `status` 10s.

**`FailureKind`** (`src/core/errors.ts:2-18`):
```ts
'cliMissing' | 'cliTooOld' | 'notGit' | 'notEnabled' | 'notFound' | 'notLoggedIn' | 'noAccess' | 'pluginMissing' | 'timeout' | 'aborted' | 'exit' | 'spawn' | 'unexpectedOutput'
```
`Result&lt;T&gt;` is `{ok:true,value}|{ok:false,failure:{kind,message,detail?}}`. Classification happens in `processFailure` (entireCli.ts:475-516), where `spawnError` → `cliMissing`. `cliTooOld` is decided in RepoManager, not in classification.
